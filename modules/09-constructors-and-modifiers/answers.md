# Quiz answers

**1. How often does a constructor run for one deployment?**

Once, during construction.

**2. What does the underscore in a modifier represent?**

The place where the modified function body executes.

**3. Why is testing only with the deployer insufficient?**

It does not verify that unauthorized callers are rejected.

**4. Who is msg.sender if a factory deploys the contract?**

The factory contract. It is the immediate caller creating the instance.
