# Quiz answers

**1. What is the default member of an enum?**

Its first member, represented by zero.

**2. Does an enum automatically enforce transition order?**

No. Functions must validate the current state.

**3. Why add exists to a mapping of structs?**

Default fields alone may not distinguish an absent record from a legitimate record.

**4. What is a useful test after completing a task?**

Attempt completion again and verify that the invalid repeated transition reverts.
