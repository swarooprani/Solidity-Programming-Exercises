# Module 18 — Explained answers

### 1. Why specify a contract before implementation?

**Answer:** To define requirements, accepted inputs, permissions and failure behavior.

### 2. Why does the gradebook track exists?

**Answer:** Zero may be an intentionally recorded score.

### 3. What is the registration capacity invariant?

**Answer:** Total successful registrations never exceed capacity.

### 4. Does a wallet address verify a human identity?

**Answer:** No.

### 5. Are the examples audited for real-world funds?

**Answer:** No; keep them in Remix VM.
