# Module 05 — Answers with explanations

### 1. How many times does a while loop execute if its condition begins false?

**Answer:** Zero.

### 2. Which loop runs its body at least once?

**Answer:** do-while.

### 3. What does continue do?

**Answer:** Skips the rest of the current iteration and proceeds with the next iteration.

### 4. Why is looping over an unbounded growing array dangerous?

**Answer:** The gas needed may eventually exceed the transaction/block gas limit.

### 5. What does for(i=0;i<3;i++) visit?

**Answer:** Indices 0, 1 and 2.
