# Module 12 — Answers and reasoning

### 1. What keyword introduces inheritance?

**Answer:** is.

### 2. Why use virtual?

**Answer:** To permit a derived override.

### 3. What does override declare?

**Answer:** That the function implements or replaces inherited behavior.

### 4. Can an incomplete abstract contract be deployed?

**Answer:** No.

### 5. What determines super resolution?

**Answer:** The Solidity inheritance linearization.
